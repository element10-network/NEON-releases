Thank you for testing the Neon Network. This box is a converted Helium hotspot, now an atom: it hears the Meshtastic radios around it, passes their messages on and earns photons for the coverage it gives; atoms talk to each other through the Plasma. A test network: things change, and the NEON token is on Solana's test network (devnet).

1Plug it in

  1. Antenna first, then the rest. A radio that runs without its antenna can be damaged.
  2. A network cable to your router (best), then the power.
  3. Wait about 10 minutes the first time (up to 30 if it is freshly installed). Leave it alone meanwhile.

No cable? After 2 minutes without a network the atom opens its own Wi-Fi, Neon-Setup-…. Join it with your phone and a page opens by itself; under Connect with choose Wi-Fi, then your home network. Afterwards put your phone back on your home Wi-Fi.

2Set it up (one page)

On a phone or computer on the same network, open the panel address above. If it does not open, find the atom in your router's list of devices and open http://ADDRESS:8080.

  1. Network connection: Cable or Wi-Fi.
  2. Atom name: how other operators see it.
  3. Modem preset: the one the Meshtastic users near you use. Ask your local Meshtastic group: it often differs from one area to the next. With the wrong preset the atom hears nothing.
  4. Panel password: twice. Write it down somewhere safe (not on this sheet).
  5. Transmit and Join the Neon network: leave them on.
  6. Payout wallet: Connect wallet with a Solana wallet in your browser (Solflare or Phantom) and sign the message. It is free: nothing is sent to the blockchain. Your photons go to this wallet.
  7. Position: drop the pin where the atom stands. Others see it rounded to about 1 km.
  8. Save and start. The atom restarts and asks for the password.

3Day to day

  • Atom: photons today and this week; Claim NEON after each weekly epoch (Tuesdays).
  • Plasma: messages with other operators and the # Earth channel.
  • Meshtastic: what the atom hears on the radio.
  • Updates install by themselves at night (03:00–05:00 UTC).

4Keep it running

  • Leave it on and online. When it is silent for 30 minutes, you get a message on the Plasma.
  • Outdoors or by a window, antenna upright: it hears more and earns more.
  • Unplugging is fine; it starts again by itself.

Notes

If something is wrong

  • No page: wait 10 minutes after power-up, then try http://ADDRESS:8080.
  • Meshtastic stays empty for an hour with users nearby: check the modem preset with your local group (Settings → Meshtastic → Radio).
  • “Packet forwarder not sending” at the bottom: restart with the round arrow at the top right, or unplug the power for 10 seconds.
  • Forgot the password: write to us.
  • Anything else: a screenshot of the page to the address below.

What the atom shares

  • With the electrodes (the network's servers): a fingerprint and the signal of the packets it hears, not their contents; its position as you share it; its wallet.
  • With every atom: its name, version, shared position, wallet, and your profile if you set one.
  • Direct messages between operators are end-to-end encrypted. Your password and wallet keys never leave your devices.
Giving it back or passing it on: with the atom online, Settings → Reset atom, type RESET and confirm. It retires its key on the network and wipes your settings, messages, wallet and Wi-Fi. Then hand it over with this sheet.
